# ReplyRush

## Turn customer reviews into better business decisions.

**AI-powered review and complaint management for small local businesses.**

ReplyRush reads what a customer actually wrote, drafts a specific and honest reply in seconds, prioritizes urgent feedback, and shows the owner which problems keep repeating across customer reviews.

Instead of treating every review as an isolated message, ReplyRush helps businesses move from:

**Read → Reply → Forget**

to:

**Understand → Respond → Identify Patterns → Take Action**

---

## Problem

Small business owners receive reviews and complaints on platforms such as Google, Zomato, Amazon, Yelp and Instagram, but rarely have enough time to answer each one personally or analyze what customers are repeatedly complaining about.

This creates several problems:

- Unanswered negative reviews can damage trust with future customers.
- Copy-pasted templates ("we're sorry for the inconvenience") can make customers feel that nobody actually read their review.
- Feedback is scattered across different platforms, so recurring problems such as slow delivery, rude staff or order issues can go unnoticed.
- Business owners may spend valuable time manually reading reviews and writing repetitive responses.
- Generic AI writing tools may invent refunds, discounts, promises or other actions that the business never approved, creating potential problems for the business.
- Responding to individual complaints does not necessarily reveal the underlying operational problem affecting multiple customers.

The result is that businesses may be **responding to customer complaints without fully understanding the patterns behind them.**

---

## Solution

### ReplyRush

ReplyRush provides a single workspace for businesses to organize, analyze and respond to customer feedback from different platforms.

It combines **AI-assisted response generation** with **complaint-pattern detection**.

The workflow is:

1. **Connect** a platform and see its reviews, with the most urgent ones first.
2. **Generate** a reply written from the actual review text, in the tone and business context the owner chooses.
3. **Edit and copy** the draft, then paste it wherever the review lives.
4. **Watch Issue Radar** to identify complaint themes that keep repeating.
5. **Inspect the underlying reviews** behind a recurring issue.
6. **Use the suggested action** to understand what the business could investigate or improve.

The goal is not simply to generate more replies.

The goal is to help a small business understand:

> **"What are my customers repeatedly telling me, and how should I respond?"**
